I'm Dominique-Savio SAKOI, a.k.a. **Dmod**. Junior Security Engineer with an offensive mindset, CTF player and privacy advocate, I spend a lot of time breaking, understanding and protecting systems. This blog is where I share what I learn along the way.

<!--more-->

## What you'll find here

- **CTF writeups**: how I solved a challenge, what worked, what didn't, and the lesson to take from it.
- **Offensive security**: web exploitation techniques, cryptanalysis, reverse engineering, always within a legal and authorised scope.
- **Defense**: hardening, detection, incident response, and the making of [Hydra](https://sakoi-cyber.me/hydra.html), our autonomous cyber-defense for French-speaking Africa.
- **Privacy**: the tools and habits I use daily to protect my data, and why.

## Following the blog

The simplest way is to subscribe to the [RSS feed](/feed.xml) with any RSS reader. No tracking: you get the articles, nothing more. You can also subscribe by email from the home page.

To reach me or see my projects, head to my [portfolio](https://sakoi-cyber.me).

See you soon for the first real article.
